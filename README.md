## NextIQ

NextIQ is an AI-powered application built inside ERPNext that helps sales teams capture business card information and automatically create lead records in ERPNext.

### What is NextIQ?

NextIQ was created by Satat Tech to solve a simple but time-consuming problem faced by sales teams: manually entering lead information from business cards into their CRM.

Traditionally, a salesperson receives a business card, looks through the details, and manually enters the name, company, phone number, email, and other information into the system. This takes time and can easily lead to data-entry errors.

With NextIQ, the process is much simpler.

Scan the business card → AI extracts the information → Lead is created automatically inside ERPNext.

### Why Was NextIQ Created?

ERPNext is open source and highly customizable, which makes it possible to build solutions directly into the ERP environment.

Satat Tech created NextIQ to make lead capture faster and easier for sales teams. Instead of spending time entering business card details manually, users can simply scan a card and let NextIQ automatically capture the information and create the lead.

### Features

NextIQ combines AI and automation to simplify sales data entry and follow-up.

- **Business Card Scanning**: Scan a business card and automatically extract relevant contact information.
- **Automatic Lead Creation**: Create leads automatically inside ERPNext without manually entering card details.
- **Frappe CRM & ERPNext CRM**: Leads can be created in either Frappe CRM or ERPNext CRM, depending on the user's requirements.
- **Notes**: Add notes to leads to keep important information and context in one place.
- **Voice Notes**: Capture information using voice notes instead of typing everything manually.
- **Automated Tasks & Events**: Create tasks and events for leads to help sales teams manage follow-ups and next steps.
